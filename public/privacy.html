Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MentorTrack. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website and tell you about your privacy rights.

2. The Data We Collect

MentorTrack operates with a "Zero-Friction" philosophy. We collect the absolute minimum amount of data required to provide our service:

  • Mentor Data: Name, email address, and an encrypted password used solely for account authentication.
  • Student Data: Public handles for LeetCode and GitHub provided by the mentor. We do NOT collect or require passwords, OAuth tokens, or private emails from students.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the provided public handles to fetch entirely public coding statistics from LeetCode and GitHub APIs. This data is displayed strictly on the mentor's private dashboard to facilitate mentorship.

4. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorised way. Passwords are cryptographically hashed, and access to dashboards requires a secure JSON Web Token (JWT).

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on public APIs provided by LeetCode and GitHub. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms. We do not sell or share your data with any third-party advertisers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at via the form on our homepage.
